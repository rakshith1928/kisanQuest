# KisanQuest Design System

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED for STITCH):**
- Platform: Mobile-first
- Vibe: Gamified, RPG, immersive, vibrant agricultural themes, accessible to rural users. 
- Palette: 
  - Earthy Green (#388E3C for primary actions)
  - Golden Yellow (#FBC02D for coins/rewards)
  - Warm Dirt Brown (#5D4037 for panels)
  - Soft Sky Blue (#BBDEFB for backgrounds/weather)
- Typography: Large, highly legible, minimal text. (Inter or Roboto)
- Styles: Extra rounded corners (ROUND_EIGHT or higher), high elevation/shadows for interactive elements.
- Notes:
  - Keep text minimal; rely on large icons and illustrations.
  - Buttons should be large and tap-friendly.
  - Financial themes should be visually clear (e.g. red for debt, green for cash).
