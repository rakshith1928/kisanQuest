A player dashboard screen showing long-term progress in the farming RPG.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Mobile-first
- Vibe: Rewarding, educational, gamified, tactile. 
- Palette: Earthy Green (#388E3C), Golden Yellow (#FBC02D), Warm Dirt Brown (#5D4037), Soft Sky Blue (#BBDEFB).
- Ambient Depth: High elevation for central interaction elements.
- No-Line rule: Soft tonal bounding boxes.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Header:** Title "Player Profile" with a stylized avatar/farmer icon.
2. **Financial Health Score:** A large circular progress indicator (donut chart) showing a health score out of 100, colored Earthy Green.
3. **Badges & Milestones:** A grid or horizontal scroll of unlocked circular badges (e.g., "First Harvest", "Debt Free"). Gamified high-elevation chips.
4. **Season History Timeline:** A vertical timeline UI showing past seasons (e.g., Season 1: +₹5k, Season 2: -₹2k) with small icons.
5. **Leaderboard Block:** A snapshot of top 3 players locally/regionally, presented as a soft-edged card.
